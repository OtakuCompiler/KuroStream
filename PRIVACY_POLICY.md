# KuroStream Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 1, 2026**

## Data We Collect
- **Watch history**: Stored locally on your device, encrypted with AES-256.
- **Search queries**: Processed locally, never sent to our servers.
- **Crash reports**: Anonymous technical data sent via Firebase Crashlytics.
- **Extension usage**: Anonymous analytics to improve source recommendations.

## Data We Do NOT Collect
- We do NOT collect your name, email, phone number, or address.
- We do NOT collect payment information.
- We do NOT sell data to third parties.
- We do NOT track you across apps or websites.

## Third-Party Services
- **Firebase Crashlytics**: Anonymous crash reporting.
- **Firebase App Check**: App integrity verification.
- **Google Cast**: Optional Chromecast functionality.

## Your Rights
- Request data deletion: Settings > Privacy > Delete All Data.
- Export data: Settings > Privacy > Export Watch History.
- Contact: privacy@kurostream.app

## Data Retention
- Watch history: Until you delete it or uninstall the app.
- Crash logs: 90 days.
- Analytics: 26 months.
